Probability distributions and how they are related

This site is an interactive reference for the probability distributions used most in statistics and computational modeling, and for the relationships between them. Each distribution has its own page with an interactive plot, its formulas, its properties and its uses.

On the map below, each tile is a distribution with a small sketch of its shape. Click a tile to open that distribution's page. Each arrow is a relationship, such as a special case, a limit or a transformation, and its label gives the key step as a short formula. Click an arrow or its label to read an explanation. Drag to move the map, and use the buttons in its corner to zoom.
