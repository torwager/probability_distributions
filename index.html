Probability distributions and how they are related

This site describes the probability distributions most used in statistics and computational modeling. Each distribution has a page where you can change its parameters by dragging handles on a plot, and see its formulas, properties and uses. Relationships between distributions, such as special cases, limits and transformations, each have their own page.

The interactive map of relationships is being built. For now, the distributions are listed below by family; the relationships are listed on their own page.
